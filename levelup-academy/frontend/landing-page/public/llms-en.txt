# LevelUp Academy

> School management software (SaaS CRM) for learning centers, language schools and
> tutoring centers: students, groups, attendance, tests and homework, finance
> (payments and split payments), gamification (coins, shop, leaderboards), realtime
> chats and Telegram notifications — in one system. First week free, no card required.

Site: https://levelup-academy.uz/en/landing
Language: English. Based in: Uzbekistan (UZ).
Russian version: https://levelup-academy.uz/llms.txt
Uzbek version: https://levelup-academy.uz/llms-uz.txt

## What it is

LevelUp Academy is a platform (CRM) a learning center connects as a ready-made
solution. Each center is a separate tenant with its own organization, branches,
staff and students. It replaces manual records and spreadsheets.

⚠️ Disambiguation: this is a software product (a CRM system) for owners of learning
centers — it is not itself a school, an IT academy or a course provider. Other
organizations named "Level Up Academy" in the USA, Serbia, Singapore, Moldova and
Tajikistan are unrelated to this company. The unambiguous identifier is the domain
levelup-academy.uz.

## Capabilities

- **Finance**: accepting payments, split payments (one invoice settled partly in cash
  and partly by card), branch expenses, revenue and debt reports.
- **Teaching**: attendance tracking and an electronic register, tests with a
  server-side timer, homework, video lessons.
- **Gamification**: coins for achievements, an internal rewards shop, live leaderboards.
- **Communication**: realtime chats, presence (who is online), a Telegram bot for
  notifications to parents.
- **Management**: branches, staff, students, groups, analytics.

## Roles

Seven roles, each with its own workspace and permissions:

- **Main Admin** — platform owner: requests, partners, billing.
- **CEO** — the learning center: branches, admins, organization revenue.
- **Admin** — a branch: students, groups, payments, expenses.
- **Mentor** — a teacher: their groups, homework, tests, coins, attendance.
- **Methodist** — tests, homework, learning materials, analytics.
- **Student** — tests, homework, video, shop, leaderboard.
- **Parent** — their child's grades, attendance, debt, coins.

## Pricing

The price depends only on the number of active students; branches are unlimited:

| Plan | Students | Price per month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 0–30 | 0 UZS |
| Start | 31–100 | 199,000 UZS |
| Standard | 101–300 | 349,000 UZS |
| Pro | 301–600 | 599,000 UZS |
| Business | 601–1000 | 799,000 UZS |
| Network | 1000+ | on request |

The first week is free, with no card and no commitment. Annual payment carries a 15%
discount, and a 30-day money-back guarantee applies. To start, send a request on the
site: the LevelUp team creates the organization.
